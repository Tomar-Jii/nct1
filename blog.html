STUDENT LIFE

Why consistency matters more than last-minute study

By Aryan · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Most of us have had a day when an assignment is due tomorrow and we suddenly feel very motivated to finish it. It works sometimes, but studying everything at the last moment can be stressful. I have realised that doing a little work regularly is usually a better approach.

Small sessions are easier to manage

Instead of planning to study for five hours at once, I find it more realistic to begin with a focused 30-minute session. A short break afterwards helps me return with a clearer mind. The important part is getting started rather than waiting for the perfect mood.

Revision makes a difference

Reading a topic once does not always mean we remember it. Going over class notes, solving a few questions and explaining a concept in our own words can make learning stronger. It also helps us notice which parts need more attention.

Progress doesn't have to be perfect

There will be days when the timetable doesn't go as planned. That is normal. Instead of giving up on the whole plan, I think it is better to adjust it and continue the next day. A routine should help us, not make us feel guilty.

My takeaway is simple: regular effort may look small on a single day, but it adds up over time. Even one useful study session is a good start.

— Aryan
