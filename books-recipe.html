SOMETHING DELICIOUS

Quick homemade masala poha

A light, tasty breakfast that doesn't need too many ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 small onion and 1 green chilli
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds and a few curry leaves
  • Turmeric, salt and a little oil
  • Lemon juice, coriander and peanuts

How to make it

  1. Rinse the poha gently, drain it and let it soften for a few minutes.
  2. Heat oil, add mustard seeds, curry leaves, chilli and onion.
  3. Add turmeric, salt and peanuts. Stir for a minute.
  4. Mix in the poha and cook on low heat for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Finish with lemon juice and coriander. Serve warm!
